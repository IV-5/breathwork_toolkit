Questions, answered

Do I need any equipment?

No. You only need your nose, your breath and a phone timer. Every technique works sitting, standing or lying down.

How long does each technique take?

Between 15 seconds and 5 minutes. The 7-Day Calm Plan takes about 10 minutes a day at the start.

Is this medical advice?

No. It is a gentle self-care tool. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and does not replace your doctor or your medicine.

I have asthma or a heart condition. Can I use it?

Please check with your doctor first. The guide includes a safety page that lists who should get medical advice before starting.

How do I receive the guide?

You get an instant PDF download after checkout on Selar. It reads well on a phone, tablet or computer.

Does it teach breath holding?

No. It uses gentle nasal breathing with a longer out-breath and no breath holding.
